# Cockpit audio provenance

`assets/cockpit-loop.ogg` is the same original procedural ambient composition as
`cinematic_music_studio/assets/glass-horizon.ogg`. It was generated for this repository from
layered sine synthesis, filtering, tremolo, echo, fades, and stereo placement with FFmpeg. It has
no external samples or third-party musical composition and ships under the repository's MIT
license. The duplicate keeps the Tesla showcase self-contained when packaged or launched alone.
